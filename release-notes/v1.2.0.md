**What's new**

- **Live microphone broadcast** (experimental, off by default). Hold the steering-wheel microphone button for a second to speak through the car's exterior speaker, and press it again to stop. Settings → Car → Experimental features → Exterior speaker → Live microphone broadcast. Tried on a Leopard 5 only.
- **Lower DiLink versions.** Support for DiLink 3, 4 and 5.0 head units added, partial for now.
- **Polish and Indonesian.** The app, the assistant's replies and the car-command names are translated. Indonesian thanks to @jkabuaufa-tech.
- **Drive modes on more cars** (experimental, off by default). Eco, Normal, Sport, Snow, Mud and Sand, whichever the car has; not tried on a car yet.
- **Private logs.** A shared or saved log hides what you said, places, keys and numbers unless you choose to show them, and the app no longer keeps a copy of its log in shared storage.
- **Update notes.** The notes of the releases skipped on the way to an update are shown, newest first.

**Important fixes**

- In-app updates work on Android 10 head units (DiLink 3 and 4); before, every update was refused as a wrong signature.
- The greeting gives up after a minute of trying to connect, instead of trying on with its banner on screen.
- If the app is killed while the exterior speaker is on, the next start switches it off again.
